BRAND IDENTITY
Primary color: Purple #9106C4 (dominates 60% of compositions — hero backgrounds, primary buttons, headlines)
Secondary color: Orange #F6A138 (25% — secondary CTAs, pricing highlights, "Ahead" in wordmark)
Accent colors: Pink #F246C2, Blue #5689EF, Green/Teal #3BCC8E (15% combined — logo corners, badges, decorative elements)
Neutral dark: #0A0A0A (background for premium/dark contexts)
Off-white surface: #F5F4F0

TYPOGRAPHY
Display/Headlines: Montserrat Black or ExtraBold — used in ALL CAPS or Title Case for post headlines, hero sections, banners
Body/UI: Poppins Regular/Medium/SemiBold — all descriptive text, captions, forms, ebooks
Numbers/Prices: JetBrains Mono — Euro values, dates, statistics

LOGO USAGE
The GA monogram symbol has 4 colored corner squares: top-left Pink #F246C2, top-right Blue #5689EF, bottom-left Green #3BCC8E, bottom-right Orange #F6A138 — always preserve these. Protection zone = 1× height of the "G" letterform on all sides. Never place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid container.

VISUAL STYLE
High energy, vibrant, modern. Dark backgrounds (#0A0A0A) with vivid purple/orange contrast are the hero treatment. White backgrounds used for editorial/content-heavy layouts.
Border radius: 4px badges, 8–10px inputs/cards sm, 16px cards lg, 9999px buttons/pills.
The brand uses a repeating "GA" pattern as a decorative texture element.
Box shadows: subtle, never dramatic — 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.10)

TONE & VOICE
Warm, direct, empathetic — speaks like a knowledgeable friend, not a corporation. Always "você" (Brazilian Portuguese informal second person). Headlines: punchy, often questions or numbered lists. CTAs written in first-person reader voice ("Quero saber mais"). "Go Ahead News" sub-brand: more journalistic, informative tone.

COMPONENT PATTERNS
Buttons: rounded pill shape (border-radius 9999px), primary in #9106C4, secondary/CTA in #F6A138
Cards: white or dark (#0A0A0A) with 3px colored top border accent in brand color
Badges: small uppercase labels with 8–12% opacity background of corresponding brand color
Forms: clean, minimal — 1px border inputs with purple focus ring #9106C4

APPLICATIONS
Social: Instagram feed posts (1:1 and 4:5), Stories/Reels covers, LinkedIn posts
Digital: Landing pages, quiz/lead capture interfaces, website sections
Documents: PowerPoint decks (16:9), ebooks (A4/letter), email signatures
Print: Business cards, letterhead, cups/merch
